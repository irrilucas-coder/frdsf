# 🎬 Exemplo 07 — Luana: serviço de vídeo (versão final aprovada)

**Modelo:** Gemini Omni Flash, 10 s por cena, 9:16
**Ingredientes padrão:** foto de frente da Luana + folha de referência (`ugc/luana-folha-referencia.jpg`) + produto quando houver
**Ordem sugerida:** Flagra (gancho) → Prova de realismo → Monster → Hamburgueria → Corretores → Fechamento

Regras que resolveram os bugs:
- Nunca mencionar celular/tripé na ação (a IA desenha um telefone). Quando alguém filma, dizer "no phone visible in the frame".
- Uma posição de câmera e no máximo 3–4 ações por cena, em ordem. Giros, desvios e tela escura geram cortes, mudança de fundo e de rosto.
- Fala com tempo marcado (de X s a Y s) e nunca falando enquanto mastiga.
- "Jônatan" escrito assim para pronúncia brasileira.

## Flagra — Monster + lanche (gancho)
**Ingredientes:** Luana (frente) + folha de referência + lata de Monster

```
Vertical 9:16 realistic video, 10 seconds, ONE single continuous shot from ONE fixed camera angle, no cuts, no angle changes, no scene changes. Filmed handheld by a friend sitting across the table at her eye level, framing her from the chest up, with only a gentle natural hand shake. Clean, high-quality modern camera look: sharp detail, natural colors, warm and bright lighting. The background stays exactly the same for the whole video. No phone visible anywhere in the frame.

CHARACTER: the exact same woman from the reference images in every frame — same face, same nose, eyebrows and eyes, the small mole on her right cheek near the nose, same light olive-tan skin. Same hair the whole time: long wavy hair with a middle part, nearly black at the roots fading into caramel-brown ombré lengths. Tiny gold stud earrings, thin gold chain necklace, the same cream cotton t-shirt with the vintage print of three white daisies and two lemons on the chest. Matte natural skin with visible pores, no makeup, no plastic look. A little burger sauce on the corner of her lips.

PRODUCTS: she holds a big smash burger with both hands — brioche bun, beef patties, melted cheddar, half wrapped in brown paper, one bite already taken. On the table next to her, the exact Monster Energy can from the reference image, logo facing the camera, unchanged, cold with condensation.

SET (stays identical the whole time): a typical small neighborhood burger shop in a Brazilian community, simple, clean and well organized. White ceramic tiled wall behind her, a stainless steel counter with a flat griddle and a neat row of sauce bottles, a small glass display case, a simple menu board on the wall with no readable text, red plastic table and chairs, a paper napkin holder and a basket of fries on the table. Bright warm ceiling lights, everything clean and tidy.

ACTION (in this exact order, smooth and natural):
0–4 s: she is chewing a bite of the burger, relaxed and happy, looking down at the food.
4–5 s: she looks up and notices the camera, her eyes widen in surprise.
5–8 s: right away, smiling and embarrassed, she speaks to the friend filming.
8–10 s: she leans forward and reaches one hand toward the camera to take it, smiling; the camera moves back a little and shakes gently.

SPEECH: casual Brazilian Portuguese with a natural São Paulo accent, embarrassed and playful, smiling while talking, natural and human. Speech from 5 s to 8 s: "Ai, para de gravar! Eu tô toda suja… me dá isso aqui!" Accurate lip sync, natural blinking.

AUDIO: her voice, a short natural laugh at the end, soft sizzling from the griddle, quiet burger shop ambience. No music.

AVOID: no cuts, no angle changes, no background changes, no sudden laughs out of order, no phone visible, no face change, no hair change, no subtitles, no on-screen text, no changes to the can design, no changes to the t-shirt print.
```

As demais cenas (prova de realismo, Monster, hamburgueria, corretores e fechamento) seguem as versões finais enviadas na conversa, com as mesmas regras acima.
