# 🧴 Exemplo 03 — Produto na mão (modelo para qualquer produto)

**Objetivo:** vender um produto físico (exemplo: creme hidratante — troque `[NOME DO PRODUTO]` e as falas)
**Duração:** 4 cenas × 10 s ≈ 36 s (Omni Flash) — ou 4 × 8 s ≈ 30 s (Veo 3.1 Fast, falas "versão 8 s")
**Formato:** vertical 9:16 · **Personagem:** JU · **Produto:** sim — **você manda a foto do produto** no Nano Banana e no Flow

---

## 1. Prompt da personagem — Nano Banana

> Candid smartphone selfie photo, vertical 9:16, taken with the front camera of an iPhone held at arm's length, slightly above eye level, 24mm wide-angle perspective. A 34-year-old Brazilian woman with deep brown skin with warm undertones, high cheekbones, a wide genuine smile with slightly uneven teeth, dark brown eyes. Hair: voluminous natural 4A coily black hair in a high puff with soft defined edges, a few loose coils framing the face, natural shine on the coils. Real skin texture: visible pores on the nose and cheeks, natural sheen on the forehead and nose bridge, slight hyperpigmentation spots on the cheeks, fine smile lines, soft under-eye shadows, natural lip texture. Bare skin with only mascara and clear lip balm. Small gold hoop earrings. Wearing a mustard oversized cotton t-shirt with natural creases. Expression: happy relaxed smile, mouth slightly open as if mid-sentence, looking straight into the lens. Setting: her bathroom, white tiles, a sink with a few skincare bottles and a toothbrush cup softly out of focus. Lighting: warm vanity light from above mixed with soft daylight from a small window camera-left. Smartphone photo look: mild digital noise in the shadows, phone HDR processing, focus on the eyes, background naturally soft. Unretouched, no beauty filter, no airbrushing, no text, no watermark.

## 2. Prompt de composição com o produto — Nano Banana

Anexe: **imagem 1 = JU**, **imagem 2 = foto do produto**.

> Using image 1 (the person) and image 2 (the product): create a candid smartphone selfie photo, vertical 9:16, of the exact same woman from image 1 — same face, skin texture and tone, same coily hair puff, same mustard t-shirt and gold hoops — holding the exact product from image 2 in her right hand next to her cheek, label facing the camera. The product keeps its exact shape, colors, proportions, logo and label text from image 2, sharp and readable, unchanged. Natural relaxed grip with fingers on the sides and bottom, not covering the label, realistic short natural fingernails. Same bathroom setting with warm vanity light and soft window daylight, natural shadows and slight reflections on the packaging. Smartphone photo look, mild noise, unretouched, no text added, no watermark.

## 5. Adaptando para outro produto

- **Comida/bebida:** troque a demonstração por *she takes a bite / takes a sip and reacts with closed eyes*; o local vira cozinha ou o próprio estabelecimento.
- **Eletrônico/acessório:** a cena 3 mostra o uso (*she plugs it in, the light turns on*), câmera traseira em close.
- **Roupa/calçado:** cena 3 vira espelho de corpo inteiro (*full-length mirror selfie, rear camera*).
- Evite prometer cura, emagrecimento ou resultado garantido na fala.
